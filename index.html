2026-2027 Ultimate SSSD Master Schedule

How to use this:
  1. Use the tabs above to jump straight to one school (still filterable by Day, Grade, Subject, Period type), see everyone at once on "Master Schedule," or check the "CTM/PLC/Planning Overview" tab for a grouped view across buildings.
  2. Pick a Building, Day type, Grade, Subject, and/or Period type from the dropdowns to narrow the view - each dropdown lets you check as many options at once as you want (e.g. two buildings, three grades), not just one.
  3. Each row is one teacher; each colored block is one period, with its exact start/end time printed on it.
  4. The colored left edge groups teachers on the same team (grade level or department).
  5. Scroll right to see the full day - teacher names stay locked on the left.
  6. Hover any block for full details (room, subject, activity). Use Reset to clear all filters.
  7. Click any block to add it to "My Schedule" and export a calendar file - and this page remembers your last tab and filters, so it reopens the way you left it.

How to use this:
  1. Every CTM, PLC, and Prep/Planning block across the district, grouped so instructional coaches can see conflicts at a glance.
  2. Elementary (K-5) is grouped by grade level across every building, since coaches often follow a grade regardless of which school it's in.
  3. Middle/High/Panther (6-12) is grouped by subject across all three secondary buildings, since most 6-12 coaches specialize in one subject.
  4. Use the toggle below to show just one grade band or both at once, depending on which grades you coach.
  5. Colors match the same team colors used on the Master Schedule tab.

Each block shows its start-end time and activity. Striped blocks with a * = exact time not provided by the school; shown in period order at an estimated 50-minute slot so periods can still be compared side by side. Blocks with a dashed border and a † = a team-shared block (some elementary grade-level teams share most of the day; the shared block is copied to every teammate from their team's schedule, while each teacher's own individually-listed blocks like PE stay unique to them). Each teacher's row has a colored left edge and a small header grouping same-color rows together - the color marks their grade level (elementary) or department (secondary) so you can see at a glance who's on the same team. When more than one building is selected, each lane is labeled Teacher (Building). Subject is inferred from each activity's name/department and may need spot-checking for ambiguous entries (e.g. bare "Tier 2/3" blocks are assumed to be reading intervention). Click any block to add it to "My Schedule" (top right) - you can rename each pick and export the whole list as a calendar file to import into Google Calendar, Outlook, or Apple Calendar.
